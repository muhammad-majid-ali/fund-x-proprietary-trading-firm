
# FUND X – Proprietary Trading Firm

### From a University Business Idea to a Digital Project

## About the Project

Fund X is a proprietary trading firm concept developed as part of a university project. The idea focuses on providing skilled and disciplined traders with access to trading capital through structured evaluation programs and professional risk management.

This repository documents the original business concept, university presentation, and website development journey of Fund X.

## Vision

To become a globally trusted proprietary trading firm known for integrity, transparency, and disciplined traders.

## Key Features

- Funded trading accounts ranging from $5,000 to $100,000
- Structured trader evaluation programs
- Professional risk management
- MetaTrader 5, MatchTrader, and cTrader platform concepts
- Flexible reward cycles
- Trader support and development
